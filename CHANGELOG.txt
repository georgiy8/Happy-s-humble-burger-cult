HHBC mod - change log (newest entries at the end)
====================================================

HHBC mod - пакет "данные + автоприём в двух режимах"
Распаковать поверх проекта (с заменой). Release | x64 -> version.dll -> в папку игры.

Изменено: dump.cpp, spy.cpp, features.cpp, overlay.cpp, dllmain.cpp, common.h
Что в меню (Insert):
  Orders  - Auto-accept: сам выбирает режим (хост/соло или клиент), показывает последний режим.
  World   - кнопка FULL DUMP (внутри смены!) -> hhbc_details/kitchen/recipes/world .txt в папке игры.
  Debug   - Spy ON + кнопки MARK: grill/board/drink/fryer/oven/bag (метки в логе).
  Kitchen - чек-лист, что делать руками.

==== UPDATE 5 ====
Kitchen tab = auto-cook lists (jobs). Each click starts an independent list; they run in parallel.
  Burgers: one button per raw patty type found in the boxes ("Auto-cook: Raw Beef Patty", ...).
           take -> throw on grill (grill switched on if needed) -> wait COOKED -> inventory (default) or board.

  Sides:   cookies+pies batch (2 trays, 2 oven slots), french fries, nuggets (fryer baskets),

Every step is written to the Spy console / hhbc_mod.log as "MOD >> ..." (Spy ON shows the game's own reaction too).

=== UPDATE 7 (patty / inventory fix) ===
Bug: a patty taken for "Auto-cook" stayed in the inventory (a "phantom" slot) although it had been thrown onto the grill.
Cause: the mod let go of the object with GenericObject.LocalDrop(), which does not always free the player's hotbar slot
(ScytheNetworkPlayer.gameObjectsInInventory), and a patty that was stashed (not the active item) could not be dropped at all.
Fix (lab.cpp, used by EVERY throw: grill, board, bag, fryer, oven, drink/shake machines, sauces):
- before the drop: if the item is stashed -> SwitchToSlot(its slot);
- after the drop: the slot is freed with GenericObject.RemoveFromHotbar(), then NotifyHotbarItemRemoved(), and only as a last
  resort the slot is cleared by force + RefreshHotbarUI. Every step is in hhbc_mod.log ("hotbar slot freed ...", "still in hotbar=0/1").
- patty on the grill: the job moves on only when the grill tracks the patty AND the inventory slot is free (otherwise it frees it / throws again).
- destination "assembly board": the job now checks that the board really got the patty (board ingredient count / object consumed),
  throws again up to 3 times and reports honestly instead of "DONE" when nothing happened.
- a patty job that fails before cooking puts the raw patty down next to you instead of leaving it in the inventory.
- other fixes: waits 4.5 s (was 2.5 s) for an item to appear in the hotbar on a laggy server; after an internal error the hand lock is reset.
Destination toggles are unchanged: "Done patty -> inventory" (default) / "-> assembly board" / both off = stays on the grill.

=== UPDATE 8 ===
1. SHAKE: the cup was always slid to the right (blueberry) nozzle. Now: left nozzle (red, strawberry) = cup position 0,
   right nozzle (blue, blueberry) = cup position 1. The job reads ShakeMachine.anchorIndex, slides the cup only if it is not under
   the wanted nozzle (each press on the cup moves it 0 <-> 1), re-checks it before and during the pour.
2. NEAREST STATIONS: every job remembers where YOU stood when it was started (all sub-jobs of ALPHA inherit that point) and picks
   everything nearest to it: ingredient boxes, grill, fryer (+ its trigger / knob / baskets), oven (+ triggers), drink machine
   (+ its buttons), shake machine, cup dispensers, dump stations, the assembly board, the sauce dispensers and the BAG.
   A shake / burger / soda therefore goes into the bag next to you, not into some other bag on the map.
3. ORDER SOURCE: ALPHA / "Shake of the accepted order" / "Soda of the accepted order" read the orders that were ACCEPTED
   (OrderManager.activeOrders, oldest first), not the text on the register. An order that alpha already builds is not taken twice
   (a failed alpha releases its order). Only if no accepted order is available the register text is used as a fallback.
4. PATTY -> BOARD: a cooked patty lying on the grill cannot be thrown (LocalDrop only works for a HELD item), so before the
   throw it is taken into the hand (HotbarGrab) and thrown from there, like a player does. The board is watched every tick; the
   patty is re-thrown only after ~1.1 s without effect. Faster waits everywhere (60 ms job tick, boxes / board / sauces / bag polled
   every tick instead of waiting a fixed time).
5. Found in the log: "could not take the cooked patty" happened when the hand was busy (fire extinguisher in use) or when the patty was
   already taken by something else - the job now says what happened and counts "patty used by the board / bag" as success.

=== UPDATE 10 ===
1. FRYER: lifting the basket is only the first press. A second press on the lifted basket empties it into the dump station
   (FryerBasket.RetrieveBasketInternal, seen in the spy log). The job now does both: lift -> retrieve -> checks that the basket's "filled" flag cleared.
   If the game lifted the basket by itself the job goes straight to the retrieve press.
2. ORDERS: accepted orders are told apart by the CustomerOrder OBJECT (not id/content), so the same order coming 5 times is built 5 times.
   An order is "taken" until it leaves OrderManager.activeOrders. Soda/shake "of the accepted order" use the oldest order alpha is not building.

=== UPDATE 11 (backrooms) ===
Alpha now understands the BACKROOMS recipes (ids from the dump): 'Raw Burger' / 'Raw Pork|Fish|Chicken Patty' = an UNCOOKED patty taken from its box and
thrown straight onto the board; 'Burnt Burger' = a patty that is left on the grill until it BURNS (Meat state 2) and then goes to the board;
'Cooked Burger' / 'Pork Patty' / 'Cooked Fish|Chicken Patty' = cooked as before. (Before, raw patties were cooked and 'Raw Burger' stopped alpha.)
Ingredient throws on the board: 2.5 cm lower (0.425 m), on a retry the target shifts 1-3 cm left/right/forward/back, up to 6 tries; a throw
counts as done also when the board consumed the object (the ingredient list is sometimes late). Finished soda: the cup we threw in is also accepted
if it was renamed into the drink.

=== UPDATE 12 (BETA) ===
LAB TAB CLEANED UP: the old numbered steps and the box list are gone. The tab now has BETA CLOSEOUT, ALPHA COOKING, CANCEL JOBS, the job list and the log tail.
New key bind (Keys tab): "Beta closeout (after-shift duties)".

BETA CLOSEOUT looks at the closeout duties that are ACTIVE and not complete (CloseoutDuty.dutyActive / dutyComplete) and does them one by one,
with the local player's own actions only (no NPC methods). Every step is written to Spy as "closeout: ...". At the start it lists the active duties.
  - Thermostats: reads targetTemp / actualTemp, presses the up / down ThermostatButton every 0.45 s until isMatched. If the value moves the wrong way
    the buttons are swapped automatically.
  - Rat traps: RatTrap.Interact on every triggered trap.
  - Turn off appliances: presses the fryer knob (FryerControl) / oven knob (Top/BottomKnobControl) until the state is Off.
  - Trash bags, kitchen trash, grease buckets: HotbarGrab, throw above the nearest TrashDisposalZone, wait until the object is gone / completed;
    a retry uses the next zone and a slightly shifted point.
  - Restock: every RestockBox is thrown into the StorageZone whose name matches the box best (names are written to Spy), retries use the next zone.
  - Napkins: NapkinBox thrown onto the dispenser that needs a refill. Air filters: a clean AirFilterItem thrown onto the AC unit without a filter.
    (never seen in a real run - guessed from the class dump; Spy shows what happens)
  - Fires (EXPERIMENTAL): takes the FireExtinguisher, calls Use() and sends the spray ray (ServerUpdateSprayRay) down onto each fire until it is extinguished.
A task that fails 3 times is switched off for the run, a single object that fails 5 times is skipped. The job ends when all duties are complete
(or after 25 s with nothing the mod can do, listing what is left). CANCEL JOBS stops it.


=== UPDATE 13 (BOOK MENU) ===
The menu is now a paper book. Two images are embedded in the DLL as resources (hhbc_ui.rc, ui_book.png = the book page, ui_btn*.png / ui_sl_*.png = button and slider pieces).
ADD hhbc_ui.rc and all ui_*.png (15 files) to the project folder (they are in the zip); the vcxproj already has <ResourceCompile Include="hhbc_ui.rc" />.
The six bookmarks are the pages: Player, Orders, World, Kitchen, Lab, Debug / Keys. Left page = controls, right page = status, jobs, log, key binds.
The whole book is drawn in 1536x1024 book pixels and scaled to the window (GUI.matrix), so it fits any window size. Selected toggles are the dark button.
If a texture cannot be created (resource missing, ImageConversion module stripped, a method renamed) the log says "book: ..." and the old grey menu is used.

UPDATE 13b: the game strips unused engine methods (DrawTextureWithTexCoords, Button/3, HorizontalSlider/6 do not exist). The book now draws whole piece textures with GUI.DrawTexture, and the real
GUI.Button / slider are drawn fully transparent (GUI.color alpha 0) just to catch the mouse. If a needed method is missing the log lists all GUI methods ("book-diag: ...").

UPDATE 14: the bookmarks are now their own image (ui_tab.png) drawn BEHIND the page; the selected one slides up and is bright, the others are darker (lighter on hover). Book without tabs = new ui_book.png.

UPDATE 17: ALPHA COOKING
  - A failed part of the order is now REPEATED instead of skipped: drinks / shakes / fries / nuggets / trays / bag jobs (up to 8 repeats per order),
    burger tasks (ingredient, sauce, patty, bagging the finished burger: up to 3 repeats each). Every repeat is written to Spy ("REPEATING").
    Only if the repeats run out the item is skipped and the bag is left OPEN (so a wrong order is never handed over).
  - When everything is in the bag the mod now CLOSES it (BaggingSystem.FinishBag, the same call as the bag bell), finds the new "To-Go Bag",
    takes it and throws it onto the FulfillmentWindow whose type matches the bag (BaggedOrder.intendedWindowType == FulfillmentWindow.orderWindowType),
    retrying on the next window if needed. The job is DONE when the bag is gone. Spy lines: "FinishBag", "finished bag", "bag type=.. -> window type=..".
  - Only lab.cpp and README.txt changed.
  - Fix (17b): the selected bookmark no longer hides the text of the next bookmark (only the hovered one slides over its neighbours). overlay.cpp changed too.

UPDATE 18 (fixes from the log of beta 17)
  - Soda: finished drink is recognised by the CANONICAL drink name ("Swimmers Choice"); the order text says "Swimmer's Choice" (with an apostrophe) and
    it never matched, so an EMPTY cup lying next to the machine was taken as the drink. Empty cups are now never taken as a finished drink.
  - Soda cups are made strictly one after another: the next cup is taken only when the previous drink has left the machine ("machine did not take the cup" fixed).
  - If a drink bag job still holds an empty cup, the drink is made again instead of throwing the cup into the bag over and over.
  - A failed bag job puts the wrong item down next to the player (it no longer stays in the hotbar).
  - Burnt patties: patties of the 2nd, 3rd... burger of an order start cooking only when the previous burger has taken its last patty (they used to wait on the
    grill and burn). If a waiting patty burns anyway it is taken off, thrown into the trash and a NEW patty is cooked (repeat logic of update 17).
  - Only lab.cpp and README.txt changed.
